Updraft Aces

A sky shooter with a pilot and a prop plane

Break the formations

Fly a prop plane up through fantasy skies. Cloud Harbor crosses islands and a sea of clouds. Amber Canyon crosses red rock at sunset. Aurora North crosses a frozen sea at night under the northern lights. Steer, let auto-fire do the shooting, break whole formations for power-ups and roll out of trouble. No bosses. A hit costs shield, never progress.

What kind of game is this?

Formations as puzzles. Lines, V shapes, loops and swoops. Shoot down every plane in one for a bonus, and some leave a power-up behind.
Three stages. Cloud Harbor over islands and sea, Amber Canyon over red rock and a river, and Aurora North over a frozen sea at night. Each one opens the next.
The roll. Tap Roll and nothing can hit you for a moment, and your guns keep firing. Roll through bullet walls. Three rolls, refilled at every checkpoint.
Never lose progress. No lives and no game over. A broken shield sends you back to the last checkpoint with your score kept.

Things to do

  • Fly three stages: Cloud Harbor, Amber Canyon and Aurora North. Each has six sections and runs about three minutes.
  • Dodge the flak from Amber Canyon's bombers. Each shell bursts into a ring of bullets. Shoot both bombers in a pair for a bonus.
  • Pop Aurora North's balloon mines from far away. Fly too close and one blinks red, then bursts into a ring of bullets.
  • Rescue castaways on rafts by flying over them. Try Score Attack from the Menu: 2 minutes for your best score.
  • Earn four medals on each stage: Sweep, Untouched, Daredevil and Top Ace. Break formations back to back for a chain bonus.
  • Pick up pink Speed power-ups to fly faster. Catch gold coins while they flash bright for 1000 points.
  • Shoot down whole formations for a bonus and their power-ups: Spread shot, Wingmen, Shield and Speed.
  • Let your two wingmen fly beside you. Each one takes a bullet for you.
  • Roll through bullet walls and streams of bullets. Your guns keep firing.
  • Beat your best score and your best combo. Clear Cloud Harbor on Ace.
  • Earn every achievement and check your stats in Records.
  • Pick Breeze, Standard or Ace in Settings, plus five assists: extra shield, slower enemies, softer hits, more checkpoints and a glow on bullets coming at you.

Controls

Keyboard
WASD or arrows fly. Auto-fire is on. J or Space fires when Auto-fire is off. K or Shift rolls. Esc opens the Menu.
Xbox controller
Left stick or D-pad flies. A fires when Auto-fire is off. B rolls. Menu or View opens the Menu. In menus A chooses and B goes back.
Touch
Drag anywhere to fly. The plane follows your finger's movement, so your thumb never covers it. Tap Roll at the bottom right. Menu at the top right. In the Android app, Settings > Tilt to steer flies the plane by leaning the phone.

Play with an Xbox controller

Any Xbox Wireless Controller with Bluetooth works. That is every Series X|S controller and the Xbox One controllers made since 2016. If the plastic around the Xbox button matches the face of the controller, it has Bluetooth. Other controllers that your phone or browser sees as a standard gamepad use the same buttons.

On Android
  1. Hold the Xbox button to turn the controller on.
  2. Hold the small Pair button on top for 3 seconds, until the Xbox button flashes fast.
  3. On your phone, open Settings, then Connected devices, then Pair new device.
  4. Tap Xbox Wireless Controller. The Xbox button stays lit when it is connected.
  5. Open Updraft Aces. The touch buttons hide as soon as you use the controller.
In your browser
  1. Connect the controller to your computer with a USB cable or Bluetooth.
  2. Open Play in your browser. Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all work.
  3. Press any button once after the game loads, so the browser sees the controller.
Menus
The D-pad or left stick moves between buttons. A chooses and B goes back. You can play the whole game with only the controller.

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android on this page.
  2. Open the file. Allow installs from your browser if your phone asks.
  3. Tap Install, then Open.
  4. Version 0.14.0 installs over every earlier version. Your save slots stay.

Android may show a Play Protect notice for apps from outside the Play Store. Version 0.14.0 installs over the last version and keeps your saves.
