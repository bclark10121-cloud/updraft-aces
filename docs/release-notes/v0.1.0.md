# Updraft Aces v0.1.0

The first test build: a gray box of Stage 1, Cloud Harbor. The shapes are stand-ins. This build is about how flying, the formations and the roll feel.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game. The start screen shows the bearded pilot on the dock, and his plane takes off and climbs through the clouds.
2. Tap Play, then slot 1. The plane rises over the sea and the first line of planes drops in.
3. Drag anywhere to fly. The plane should follow your finger's movement, and your thumb should never cover it.
4. Shoot down a whole formation. "Formation down!" shows with a bonus.
5. Tap Roll at the bottom right. The plane flips and bullets pass through it. Three rolls, refilled at each checkpoint.
6. Take hits until the shield breaks. You go back to the last checkpoint and your score stays.
7. In Settings, try Breeze and Ace and see whether the bullets change.

## New in this version

- Vertical scrolling stage: Cloud Harbor, about 3 minutes, six sections with checkpoints, ending in a final wave section. No bosses.
- Five formation kinds: line, V, loop, swoop and the sturdy gunner plane.
- Formation bonus, score and combo (kit score), shield pips and roll charges.
- Soft fail: a hit costs a shield segment and the combo. A broken shield rewinds to the checkpoint. No lives, no game over.
- Breeze, Standard and Ace, plus Auto-fire, a smaller hitbox assist and Reduce flashing in Settings.
- Ten achievements and nine stats in Records.
- Tester note with a red marker (test builds only, behind Tester tools): F8 or the Note button freezes the screen, you draw and type, then Export notes saves one zip to attach in a chat.
