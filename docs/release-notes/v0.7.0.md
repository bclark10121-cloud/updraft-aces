# Updraft Aces v0.7.0

Ace with teeth, plus a lot more feel: buzzes, hit flashes, score pops and a clearer HUD.

## Five minute test

1. Fly Cloud Harbor on Standard. Line formations now fire three-bullet fans, and vee leaders fire five. Bullets come as soon as a formation is on screen.
2. Try Ace. Small planes fire twice, and every swooping formation comes from both sides. Expect to need your roll.
3. Roll through a bullet. A green "Close shave! +50" pops up.
4. Break a whole formation. "+1000" floats up where the last plane fell, instead of a banner over the next wave.
5. Look at the Roll button. Three pips show your charges.
6. With Rumble on in Settings, your phone buzzes on hits, rolls, formation clears and a broken shield.
7. In Amber Canyon, enemy bullets are ice blue and read well on the sand.
8. Break your shield on purpose. You go back to the checkpoint and keep Spread and Wingmen.

## New in this version

- Enemy fire patterns (idea 1): line planes fan three, vee leaders fan five, loop planes fire two quick shots, swoopers fire one. Small planes fire within a second of showing up. Breeze keeps every plane to one aimed shot.
- Ace is different, not just faster (idea 2): two volleys per small plane and mirrored swoop formations.
- The roll pays (idea 4): +50 and a pop for each bullet you roll through.
- Haptics (idea 6): buzzes on hits, rolls, formation clears and a broken shield. The Rumble switch in Settings controls them.
- Hits that feel like hits (idea 7): a white flash and a spark on each hit. "+1000" and power-up messages pop where they happen, in the game font. Big banners stay for checkpoints and stage clears.
- Canyon readability (idea 8): ice blue bullets in Amber Canyon, dark edges on your shots, and high clouds no longer cover bullets.
- HUD (idea 9): roll pips on the Roll button, a dark backing behind the shield, a bigger score, and the HUD no longer shakes with the screen. The red hurt flash is softer.
- Pressure check (idea 5): a new test fails if a stage gets too easy.
- A rewind keeps Spread and Wingmen (your question D1, set to what the game brief says).

## Judgment calls

- D1 and D2 were open. A rewind now keeps power-ups (the brief says a rewind keeps everything). Rolling still stops your guns, as the roll's cost. Say the word to flip either.
- Standard got noticeably busier. A weaving bot took 12 hits on Standard where it took 7 before. If it feels too much, the first lever is fire timing, not the patterns.
- Breeze stays gentle: one aimed shot per plane and slow bullets.
