# Updraft Aces v0.13.0

A faster plane on wide screens, a Speed power-up, gold coins and bullet walls you roll through.

## Five minute test

1. Play on a wide screen. Fly left and right. The plane crosses the screen much faster than in 0.12.
2. Clear the swoop formation at 53 seconds in Cloud Harbor. It drops a pink token with two up arrows. Catch it: "Speed up!" and a pink arrow joins the marks under your shield.
3. Shoot a gunner (the big plane). It drops a spinning gold coin. Catch it while it flashes bright for "Gold! +1000". A dim catch pays 200.
4. Around 64 seconds amber dashes flash across the top and "Wall ahead! Roll!" shows. A row of bullets fills the width. Roll as it reaches you.
5. Keep auto-fire on and roll. Your guns keep firing through the roll now.
6. On a phone, open the Menu and Settings. The rows are bigger than before.
7. Try Breeze. Its wall has a gap to fly through.

## New in this version

- Speed (BC's ask): side to side speed grows with the width of the play area. On a 16:9 screen the plane moves about 1.8 times as fast across as before. An upright phone is unchanged.
- Speed power-up: pink, two up arrows. Each one adds 18 percent, up to two levels. A third pays +1000. Two per stage. It stays through a rewind and resets on a new stage.
- Gold coins (idea 21): gunners and bombers drop a gold coin. It spins and flashes bright for a third of each second. Bright pays 1000, dim pays 200. New achievement Golden Touch (20 bright catches). Records shows "Gold caught bright".
- Bullet walls (idea 4): three per stage at 66, 112 and 158 seconds, warned 1.6 seconds ahead. Only a roll gets you through on Standard and Ace. Breeze leaves a gap. Each bullet you roll through pays a close shave. New achievement Wall Breaker.
- Your guns fire during a roll (BC's D2 answer). Power-ups stay through a rewind (BC's D1 answer, as before).
- Bigger menu rows on phones (idea 14): on a touch screen the menus grow until a row is at least 48 dp tall.
- Under the hood (idea 28): the gameplay code is split into three files. Same bot run, frame for frame, before and after.
- The checkpoint save takes 0.2 ms on a desktop (idea 32). Not yet measured on a phone.
- The APK now asks for the vibrate permission, so rumble works on Android.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.24.0. New in the kit from this game (BC said yes to both): the phone fit in `screen_fit.gd`, the title focus guard and FX2D particle helpers. Off by default for other games until their next release.

## Install

Installs over every earlier version. Save slots stay.

## Judgment calls

- Wide speed grows with width but is capped at 1.8 times. Full parity would be 2.3 times on 16:9, which felt twitchy for fine dodging. A wide crossing now takes 1.8 s against 1.4 s upright.
- Gold drops from gunners and bombers, not ground targets. The ground is a shader with no targets to shoot yet.
- Speed replaced one Spread and one Wingmen or Shield drop per stage, so the power-up count stays the same.
- Walls come right after a checkpoint refill or with time to spare, so you usually have a roll ready.
- Stage 4 waits on your picks. It comes in the next release.
