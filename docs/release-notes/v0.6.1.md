# Updraft Aces v0.6.1

Bug fixes from the four-reviewer review. Nothing new to learn, things just work right.

## Five minute test

1. If you cleared Cloud Harbor before 0.5, open that slot. Menu > Stage 2: Amber Canyon now opens.
2. Fly hard to the right edge. The plane's wing tip stays on the screen.
3. Turn the phone sideways and pick up Spread. Its marks show under your shield on the left, not under the Menu button.
4. Pick up a Shield with a full shield. You get "Shield full +1000".
5. Turn on "Assist: slower enemies". Gunners and bombers fire on the slower clock too.

## Fixed

- B1. Old saves: a player who cleared Cloud Harbor before 0.5 found Amber Canyon locked.
- B2. The "slower enemies" assist made gunners and bombers fire more. A gunner fired 11 fans with it on and 8 with it off. Now 8 and 8.
- B3. The plane could slide half off the right edge.
- B4. Spread marks hid under the Menu button on a sideways phone.
- B5. A Shield pickup at full shield did nothing but said "Shield +1".
- B6. A new save test loads a 0.1 save and round-trips Amber Canyon at a checkpoint. It fails on the 0.6.0 code, so it would have caught B1.
- Cleanup: unused code removed and the handoff notes brought up to date.
