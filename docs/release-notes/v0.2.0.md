# Updraft Aces v0.2.0

Power-ups, wingmen and the easy mode pack. Stage 1 now comes from an LDtk level file. Still gray box art.

## Five minute test

1. Play slot 1. Shoot down the whole line of planes at about 13 seconds. A yellow S drops. Fly under it: your shots fan out (Spread).
2. Clear the loop at about 37 seconds. A gold W drops. Two wingmen join you and fire with you.
3. Let a bullet hit a wingman. He peels away and your shield stays full.
4. Pick up a green S for a shield segment after a hit.
5. In Settings, turn on the five assists one by one: extra shield, slower enemies, softer hits, more checkpoints and a glow on bullets coming at you.
6. Let the shield break. You go back to the checkpoint and the power-ups are gone.

## New in this version

- Power-ups: Spread (two levels up), Wingmen (two, each takes one bullet) and Shield (+1 segment). Each wave in the stage says which power-up it drops when you clear the whole formation.
- Stage 1 lives in an LDtk level file (pilot I5). The game reads it directly, no plugin.
- Easy mode pack (studio K8, built here first): five separate assists in Settings, all off by default.
- The shield counts in half segments, so a softer hit costs half.
- Achievement Wingmates can now be earned.
