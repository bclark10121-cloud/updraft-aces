# Updraft Aces v0.9.0

A bigger plane on a sideways phone, still edge to edge.

## Five minute test

1. Turn your phone sideways and fly. Your plane, the enemies and the islands draw about a third bigger than in 0.8.
2. Fly to both edges. You still reach the left and right edges of the screen.
3. Turn back upright. Nothing changed there.

## New in this version

- Sideways phones (idea 10): the play area is shorter when the screen is clearly wider than tall (470 tall in place of 640), so everything draws bigger. It still runs the full width of the screen.
- Enemy bullets slow by the same share on the shorter area, so you get the same time to react.
- A square window, a tablet or an upright phone keeps the old layout.
- The flight test can now fly sideways (FLY_WIDE=1).

Installs over every earlier version. Save slots stay.

4. Browser: day one, Stage 2, power-ups, review on a phone-sized screen and wide screens: passed. Sideways phone screenshots compared with 0.8.

## Judgment calls

- The review suggested capping how wide the play area gets. That would undo your 0.3.1 request to fly edge to edge, so I made the area shorter instead.
- On the shorter area, formations reach you a little sooner. Bullets are slowed to match, but planes are not, so Ace sideways is a touch harder (6 hits for the bot in place of 3).
