# Updraft Aces v0.3.0

The new look and sound from your picks, plus a sea shader and particles.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game. The braided ginger pilot stands on the dock, Updraft Aces is in Titan One and Call to Adventure plays.
2. Press Play. Beachfront Celebration plays in flight. Your plane is the twin-engine with a smoke trail behind it.
3. The first formations are the olive and teal fighters. Swoopers turn to follow their loops. The big gunner shows up at about 42 seconds.
4. Shoot planes down and watch the bursts of sparks and smoke.
5. Pick up Wingmen at about 37 seconds. They are the small coral monoplanes.
6. Turn on Reduce motion in Settings. The sea holds still and the bursts get smaller.

## New in this version

- Your plane: the twin-engine. Wingmen: the coral monoplane.
- Enemies: the storm olive and teal set, a fighter for lines and V formations, a swooper for loops and swoops and the gunner.
- The pilot on the start screen. The start screen plane wears your plane's colors.
- Title font Titan One on the start screen and the game page.
- Music: Call to Adventure (start screen) and Beachfront Celebration (play), Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0, trimmed to seamless loops at -18 LUFS.
- Pilots: a sea shader with swells, glints and cloud shadows, and GPU particles for the smoke trail and bursts.
