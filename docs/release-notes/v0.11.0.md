# Updraft Aces v0.11.0

Polish: power-ups you can tell apart, a calmer sound, breathers before checkpoints and a Grand Armada finale.

## Five minute test

1. Clear a formation that drops a power-up. Spread is an orange token with three arrows, Wingmen a blue token with two small planes, Shield a mint token with a shield.
2. Listen while you fly. The shot sound is softer and plays half as often. Every 10 planes in a combo a chime plays a step higher, with a "Combo x2" pop.
3. Watch the last few seconds before each checkpoint. The sky goes quiet as the checkpoint banner shows.
4. Fly to the end of any stage. With 14 seconds left, "Grand Armada!" shows and three vee formations fly in together. Break all three for +3000.
5. On an upright phone, the start screen shows the whole pilot, and the hint text is bigger.

## New in this version

- Power-up tokens (idea 11): each has its own color and picture. None of them is the cream of your own shots any more.
- Sound (idea 12): a softer shot, played on every other volley. A combo chime climbs with your combo.
- Breathers (idea 19): no wave starts in the last 4 seconds before a checkpoint, on all three stages.
- Grand Armada finale (idea 19): three vees at once near the end of each stage. Not a boss: it flies past. New achievement: Armada.
- Start screen (idea 13): the pilot is never cut off at the left edge, and the hint text is bigger with a stronger outline.
- Lighter graphics work (idea 29): the canyon and night sea no longer draw the open sea underneath first.
- Smaller download (idea 31): the four music loops are about 2.9 MB smaller, same length and loop.

## Judgment calls

- The breather moves 9 waves (4 in Cloud Harbor, 4 in Amber Canyon, 1 in Aurora North) 4 seconds earlier, rather than removing them.
- The Armada adds 21 planes at the end of each stage. It counts toward the chain and the Sweep medal like any formation.
- Lower music bitrates can sound a touch softer on good headphones. Phone speakers won't show it.
