# Updraft Aces v0.3.1

Fly edge to edge, and your plane's wings are whole again.

## Five minute test

1. Play with the phone sideways. You can fly all the way to the left and right edges of the screen.
2. Turn the phone upright while flying. The play area fits the new shape and keeps going.
3. Look at your plane. Both wing tips and both engines show.
4. Look at the pilot on the start screen. Nothing is clipped.

## New in this version

- The play area fills the whole screen, upright and sideways. Before, it was a fixed tall strip in the middle.
- Swoop formations stretch across the wider screen.
- Fixed: the left wing tip of your plane was cut off. Fixed: a sliver of the pilot was cut off.
