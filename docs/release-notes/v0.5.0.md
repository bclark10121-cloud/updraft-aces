# Updraft Aces v0.5.0

Stage 2 is here: Amber Canyon, with bombers and new music.

## Five minute test

1. Clear Cloud Harbor. The banner says Amber Canyon is open, and the game flies on into it.
2. Look down. Red rock mesas, a stone arch, spires and a winding turquoise river scroll past at sunset.
3. Wait for the first bomber pair at about 17 seconds. Their dark flak shells blink, then burst into a ring of bullets.
4. Shoot both bombers in a pair for the formation bonus and a power-up.
5. Listen for the new music, At Launch.
6. Open the Menu. Pick Stage 1: Cloud Harbor or Stage 2: Amber Canyon.

## New in this version

- Stage 2, Amber Canyon (your pick): six sections, about three minutes. Red Gate, Arch Run, River Bend, Spire Field, Dust Shelf and Sundown Stretch. No boss.
- Bombers (your pick): big slow planes in pairs. They hold high and drop flak shells that burst into rings. They take several hits.
- At Launch by Kevin MacLeod plays in Amber Canyon (your pick). Cloud Harbor keeps Beachfront Celebration.
- Menu rows to pick a stage. Stage 2 opens after you clear Stage 1. Saved slots that already cleared Cloud Harbor have it open.
- Two new achievements: Sundown (clear Amber Canyon) and Flak Dancer (shoot down a whole bomber pair).
- Canyon pieces, sunset clouds and the bomber are painted in Gemini, in the same toy style.

## Judgment calls

- Bombers come in pairs and count as a formation, so the pair bonus and power-up drops work like the other planes.
- Flak shells hurt on contact before they burst. A wingman can take a shell for you, and a roll passes through them.
- The canyon ground is drawn by the same shader as the sea, so it costs nothing extra on phones.
- Clouds in the canyon are more solid than at sea, so they read as sunset clouds and not blue smudges over the river.
- The bot still takes no hits on Ace. Ace may need more bullets in a later release.
