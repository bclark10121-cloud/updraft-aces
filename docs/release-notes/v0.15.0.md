# Updraft Aces v0.15.0

New, sharper scenery in all three stages, the shared kit 1.25.0 (safe area and halo glow from the kit) and an HDR glow you can try on the phone.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over 0.14.0. Start Cloud Harbor. The islands are new: a palm island, a lighthouse island, a windmill island and a dock island. On the phone they are as sharp as the planes.
2. Play into Amber Canyon and Aurora North. New mesas, spires, a stone arch, icebergs, ice floes, clouds and night clouds. The balloon mine is new too.
3. Hold the phone upright and sideways. The score, the shield, Menu and Roll stay clear of the camera hole. The start screen buttons do too. The start screen picture still fills every edge.
4. Open Settings > Tester tools > Look > HDR glow and turn it On. Your shots, bursts and the Aurora North light glow brighter. Watch the frame rate readout (Settings > Frame rate). Turn it Off again if the game feels slower.
5. In the browser the game looks the same as on the phone, with the base-size pictures.

## New in this version

- A13, sharper scenery: 10 new Gemini pictures in the style thread make every island, mesa, iceberg, cloud set and the balloon mine. The browser gets them at the 0.14 sizes. The APK gets copies up to twice as sharp (34 sharper sprites, was 9). Aurora North gains a fourth night cloud.
- A16, kit 1.25.0: the safe area now comes from the kit, and it covers the start screen and every menu, not only the play HUD. The halo glow is now the kit piece `glow2d.gd`. The kit's signing script bakes the shaders, so the game's own stand-in script is gone.
- A17, HDR glow on the play field only: it runs in its own layer, so the menus and the start screen look exactly as before. Android only. Off by default: turn it on in Tester tools > Look. Battery saver and Settings > Glow switch it off.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.25.0 (new: safe area in `screen_fit.gd`, the back swipe in the starter, the shader baker in `sign_android.sh` and `check_build.py`, `glow2d.gd`). Other games take it at their next release.

## Judgment calls

- HDR glow ships off. On the software Vulkan used for testing it took a frame from about 31 ms to 54 ms. The cost is the HDR layer itself, not the glow. A real phone GPU is likely much faster, so please try it in Tester tools and tell me.
- With HDR glow on, see-through things on the field (clouds, smoke) look a little lighter. That is how HDR blends them. Menus are not affected.
- Bright things draw 1.4 times brighter in the HDR layer so they bloom. The Aurora bands were softened so they do not look like neon lines.
- Look mismatches against 0.14: the lighthouse island is bigger than before (all islands now 200 wide), the night clouds are plain slate and lost the green and violet tint, the dust clouds are pinker and lumpier, the new mine is a balloon with a small weight seen from above (0.14 had a crate seen from the side) and keeps a faint pink cast on its rim. The arch has no river painted under it any more. It sits over the shader river.
- The new keyer gives soft edges to the new scenery so no pink fringe shows. Older sprites are byte-identical.
- The browser test for wrecks now waits a moment before it reads the state. It flaked on 0.14 too.
- G0.17: this chat ran on the build model (Opus 5.5) from 10:30 AM. Build work still went to helpers.
