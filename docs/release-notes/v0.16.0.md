# Updraft Aces v0.16.0

Stage 4, Thunderhead Reach: a storm front over a dark sea, with rain, lightning, dive gliders and "Achilles" (BC's picks).

## Five minute test

1. Open the Menu. Stage 4 shows as locked until you clear Aurora North. To jump straight in, use Tester tools > Stage > Stage 4.
2. Fly Thunderhead Reach. Dark rough sea, rain slanting across the screen, sea stacks, a lighthouse islet, a reef with a wreck and a hut islet. Achilles plays.
3. Around 10 seconds a small group of dive gliders slides in and hangs. Each one blinks and shows a dashed red line to where it will dive. Step off the line, or roll through the glider for a close shave (+100).
4. Watch for lightning every 8 to 15 seconds: a short flash of the sky and sometimes a bolt inside a cloud. Turn on Settings > Reduce flashing. The flash becomes a slow, dim glow with no bolt.
5. The bullets in this stage are lime with a bright halo, so they read on the dark sea.
6. Clear the stage for the achievement Eye of the Storm.

## New in this version

- Stage 4, Thunderhead Reach: six sections (Squall Line, Lighthouse Rocks, Wreck Reef, Thunder Alley, Gale Narrows, Last Light), 40 waves, about 3 minutes. Checkpoints, rafts to rescue, bullet walls, gold, the Grand Armada fly-past, four medals and Score Attack, like the other stages. No boss.
- Dive gliders: they slide in, hang, warn with a blink and a dashed line, then dive along that line and fly off. They never shoot. Breeze gives a longer warning and a slower dive. Ace gives a shorter warning, a faster dive and one more glider per group.
- The storm: a new rough sea in both sea shaders (more foam and spray with the Android extras), rain, and lightning at most once a second. Reduce flashing softens it. Reduce motion turns lightning and rain off.
- Music: "Achilles" by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), CC BY 4.0, found through Patch.gg.
- Art from the Gemini style thread: storm rocks, islets, thunderheads and the glider. Sharper copies in the APK (43 sharp sprites).

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.25.0. No kit changes.

## Install

Installs over every earlier version. Save slots stay. An Aurora North clear from an older version opens Stage 4 when you clear Aurora North again.

## Judgment calls

- Glider balance: your plane shoots most gliders before they dive. On Standard about 14 of 48 dive in a bot run. Gliders take two hits on Standard. Tell me if you want more of them to dive (a faster slide-in, or no hits until they hang).
- A roll through a glider pays 100, twice a bullet, and counts toward Daredevil and Close Shave.
- Lime bullets with a 1.9 times halo were picked to read on the dark sea. Near the plane the halos look like soft blobs.
- At the lightning peak the thunderheads turn pale lavender for about 0.15 s.
- With the Android extras, faint surf rings circle each rock. They are softened for this stage.
- Stage 4 bullets and clouds use the same sizes as the other stages.
