# Updraft Aces v0.14.0

The Android release. Twelve Android improvements from the 2026-11-21 review: a back swipe that works, a HUD clear of the camera hole, buzz styles, Tilt to steer, sharper planes, wrecks that splash, glow, a richer sea and the Mobile renderer for the APK.

## Five minute test

Do this on the phone with the APK. Steps 6 to 8 also work in the browser.

1. Install and open it. The start screen should look the same as 0.13. If the game does not start or the picture looks wrong, tell me and install 0.13.0 again from the game page. This is the first APK on the Mobile renderer (Vulkan).
2. Start a flight. Swipe back from the edge of the screen. The Menu opens. Swipe back again. It closes. Open Settings and swipe back. Settings closes.
3. Look at the corners, upright and sideways. The score, the shield, the Menu button and the Roll button sit clear of the camera hole and the gesture bar. The sea and the planes still reach every edge.
4. Roll. You feel a light tick, white vapor leaves both wing tips and spray rises from the sea below. Take a hit: a stronger buzz. Let the shield break: a heavy thump. At the end of a stage the Grand Armada arrives with a buzz that climbs in four steps.
5. Open Settings and turn on Tilt to steer. Hold the phone the way you like and close Settings. Lean right, left, forward and back. The plane follows. Menu > Recenter tilt sets level again. Tilt strength has Gentle, Normal and Strong. Dragging still works.
6. Shoot planes down. Each wreck falls away, spins and hits the sea with a splash. Over Amber Canyon it is a dust puff. A gunner under half health trails smoke.
7. Watch your shots, the enemy bullets, the bursts and the power-ups. Each has a soft halo of light. Settings > Glow turns it off to compare.
8. Look at your plane and the enemies. They are sharper than in 0.13 (APK only).
9. Look at the sea in Cloud Harbor. Foam rings the islands, the water is turquoise near shore and a path of sun glitter runs down the screen (APK only). Turn on Battery saver. The sea goes back to the lighter one.

## New in this version

- Back swipe (review item 1): the Android back swipe or button closes whatever is open on top, or opens the Menu in play. On the start screen with nothing open it does nothing, so a stray swipe never closes the game.
- Safe area (item 2): the score, shield pill, roll pips, Menu and Roll buttons keep inside the area clear of the camera hole, rounded corners and gesture bar. The play area still runs edge to edge.
- Buzz styles (item 3): 14 events each use a named style from the kit, soft to strong. Roll and formation clear are light ticks. Pickups and rescues are a little stronger. A hit is a hit. Shield down is the heavy thump. The Grand Armada climbs.
- 120 Hz feel check (item 4): a new test proves even motion at 60, 90 and 120 frames a second.
- Sharper sprites (item 5): the plane, the wingmen, four enemies and the three rafts ship at twice the size in the APK, cut from the same Gemini pictures. The browser keeps the 0.13 sizes, so its download stays the same.
- Shadows and splashes (item 6): softer shadows under every plane. Wrecks fall and splash. The roll kicks up spray.
- Glow (item 7): soft halos of light under shots, bullets, bursts, hit flashes, power-ups and bright gold. New Settings switch: Glow.
- Richer particles (item 8): wing tip vapor on rolls, smoke on damaged gunners and bombers, smoke behind falling wrecks, round soft particles in place of squares. With the Android extras: vapor on hard turns too, thicker smoke and embers.
- Richer sea on Android (item 9): foam around islands and icebergs, turquoise shallows, a sun or moon glitter path, sparkle and a foam line on the canyon river.
- App category (item 10): Android files the app as a game.
- Mobile renderer (item 11): the APK runs Godot's Mobile renderer (Vulkan) with frame pacing set so it never locks to 30, and baked shaders so effects do not hitch the first time they show. A phone without Vulkan falls back to the old renderer by itself. The browser stays on the Compatibility renderer.
- Tilt to steer (item 12): a Settings switch on Android, off by default. Three strengths. Level is how you hold the phone when play starts, after a pause and after Menu > Recenter tilt.
- Tester tools > Look > Android extras: Follow the device, Off or On. Use it to compare the two looks on the phone.

## Judgment calls

- Glow is a layer of soft halos, not a full-screen bloom. The bloom pass cut the browser test build to a third of its frame rate and bloomed the white clouds too. The halos cost about 3 percent.
- Real HDR glow on the Mobile renderer was built, tried and left out. It blends see-through menu pieces differently, and the start screen's small text was harder to read.
- The richer sea is its own shader file (`sea_rich.gdshader`). In one file with a switch, the browser build drew at 6 frames a second on the test machine against 10 for 0.13, with the switch off. The browser sea is now the same file as 0.13.
- "Sea spray at low passes" from the review became spray under a roll. The game has no height to fly low at.
- The balloon mine has no sharper copy. Gemini painted it smaller than the game draws it. Islands and clouds are not sharper either: their sources are no bigger. Both would need new art.
- Tilt direction follows Godot's sensor rules and was checked in code only. If it flies the wrong way on your phone, tell me which way and I will flip it.
- Tilt has a small dead zone (about 2.5 degrees on Normal) so a steady hand flies straight.
- Back on the start screen does nothing. It does not quit.
- The safe area covers this game's HUD. The kit's start screen and Settings panel are not inset yet. That is a kit change and needs your yes.
- The APK on the Mobile renderer departs from E2.1 (Compatibility on both). Recorded as override 2 from your "move forward and add 1-11".
- The shader baker only works with a screen. `tools/godot_baker.sh` gives the export a virtual one. The kit's `sign_android.sh` always exports headless, so this is a kit idea too.
- Battery saver switches off the Android extras and the glow.
- Look check mismatch: at phone size the islands and clouds show stepped edges beside the sharper planes. They were the same in 0.13. New art would fix it (backlog A13).
- G0.17 (one model designs, one builds) landed at 9:35 AM on 2026-11-21, after this build had started. The design model built 0.14.0 itself. Helpers build from the next release.
