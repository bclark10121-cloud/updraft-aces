# Updraft Aces v0.12.0

Two new reasons to fly again: castaways to rescue and a 2-minute Score Attack.

## Five minute test

1. Fly Cloud Harbor. At 20 seconds a castaway on a yellow raft drifts in, with a glowing ring around it.
2. Fly over the raft and hold still. A ring fills, then "Rescued! +500". Four rafts drift by on every stage. In Amber Canyon they ride the river.
3. Open the Menu and pick "Score Attack: Cloud Harbor, 2 minutes". A clock counts down under your score.
4. Get hit until your shield breaks. In Score Attack it refills on the spot and you keep flying.
5. When time runs out you see your score and your place in your top 10. Then the stage starts again as a normal flight.
6. The Menu row shows your best Score Attack score for the stage you are on.

## New in this version

- Rescue rafts (idea 20): four castaways per stage, painted by Gemini. Hover over a raft for one second to rescue it for 500 points. New achievement Lifeline for 12 rescues. Records shows "Castaways rescued".
- Score Attack (idea 22): 2 minutes on the stage you are on, from its start. A broken shield refills with no rewind. Your top 10 for each stage saves with the slot. New achievement Against the Clock. Records shows your best Score Attack.
- New tests (idea 30): a flak shell hits the plane, a roll passes through flak, a bomber pair earns Flak Dancer and a locked stage row keeps you where you are. Plus checks for rafts and Score Attack.

## Judgment calls

- Score Attack earns no medals, since it stops at 2 minutes. Its scores do count toward your best score.
- Rescuing takes one second of hovering, and the ring drains slowly if you drift off, so a quick dodge doesn't lose your progress.
- Rafts drift with the sea, slower than the planes, so you have time to reach one.
- The bot doesn't chase rafts, so its runs show no rescues.
