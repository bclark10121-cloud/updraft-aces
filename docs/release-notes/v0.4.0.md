# Updraft Aces v0.4.0

A real app icon, painted islands and clouds, and the painted plane on the start screen.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK. The home screen icon is your twin-engine from above on sky blue with a little cloud.
2. Open the game. The pilot's plane on the dock is now the painted twin-engine, side view, with him in the canopy.
3. Play. The islands now have palms, a lighthouse, a windmill and a dock. The clouds are soft and puffy.
4. Check that bullets and enemies still read clearly over the islands and under the clouds.

## New in this version

- App icon: your pick C, repainted by Gemini with your twin-engine (the first try showed the wingman's plane). Also the game page icon and the browser tab icon. A white plane silhouette for Android's themed icons.
- Four painted islands and four painted clouds replace the shapes drawn in code.
- The start screen plane is the painted twin-engine.
- Name check: a web and store search found no game named Updraft Aces. A trademark search still comes before any store release.

## Judgment calls

- The icon art sits inside the centre two thirds on a sky blue square, so Android's round or squircle masks never cut the plane.
- High clouds stay see-through so they never hide bullets.
