# Updraft Aces v0.10.0

Stage 3 is here: Aurora North, with balloon mines and Heroic Age.

## Five minute test

1. Clear Amber Canyon. The banner says Aurora North is open, and the game flies on into it.
2. Look down. A dark night sea with green and violet aurora light on the water, icebergs, ice floes, a lighthouse berg and a snowy pine island.
3. Balloon mines drift down from about 9 seconds in. Shoot one from far away and it pops safely for 150.
4. Fly close to one. A red ring shows its reach, it blinks red and then bursts into a ring of 10 bullets. Shoot it while you're close and it bursts too.
5. Listen for the new music, Heroic Age.
6. Open the Menu. Three stage rows, each with its medals.

## New in this version

- Stage 3, Aurora North (your pick): six sections, about three minutes. Ice Gate, Floe Field, Lighthouse Point, Aurora Run, Frost Shelf and Polar Night. No boss.
- Balloon mines (your pick): groups of 4 to 6 drift down, swaying. Popped from far away they are safe. Fly within reach and they burst into a ring after a short fuse. Clear a whole group for the formation bonus and the chain.
- Heroic Age by Kevin MacLeod plays in Aurora North (your pick).
- The night sea is drawn by the sea shader, with moving aurora bands on the water.
- Enemy bullets in Aurora North are warm amber so they read on the dark water.
- Four new medals for Aurora North, and a new achievement, Northern Lights, for clearing it.
- Gemini painted the icebergs, floes, lighthouse berg, pine island, night clouds and the balloon mine in the same toy style.

## Judgment calls

- The bot shoots every mine from a safe distance, so in its runs no mine ever bursts. The mine check proves the burst on its own: a mine arms within reach, bursts after its fuse into 10 bullets, pops safely when shot from far and bursts when shot close.
- A mine that bursts counts as a plane that got away: it breaks the chain and the group's bonus.
- Mines only appear in Aurora North for now.
- Gemini drew the mine as a small hot-air balloon with a crate. It reads well from above, so I kept it.
